Role and rules as always (`.git-exclude/roles/*`, `.git-exclude/rules/*`; GUI Evidence and Test Optimization rules; Rust 2024, tests in separate files, English only, `unsafe` forbidden; **relative paths with `cd`, or none**; **gates chained with `&&`, exit status tested before any commit or push**; audit 0 / 12; CI watched after each push; no tags, force-push or history rewriting). Governing text: [RFC 034](../../accepted/034-readable-interface-for-older-users.md): requirements 1 and 4, decisions **D1** (body 16, secondary 14, caption 13 as the floor and only for text nobody needs in order to act, headings 20 and 28), "Decisions 2026-11-22", "Result of J21", and RFC 026's review note (the Result screen's scrolling middle must keep room). Review of J21b: `.git-exclude/reviewed/rfc-034-j21b-marks-review-2026-11-24.md`.

Owner philosophy: clean, safe and secure, robust design; users must not be confused. The owner asked whether the interface suits older users; **text size and target size are the two things an older reader and a less steady hand notice first.** This is the largest step of RFC 034: it **will move pixels** (text grows, controls grow). The headless matrix is the guard; the pictures are for the architect's eyes.

## What the numbers are today (architect's count)

`.size(...)` in `crates/app/src/ui` and `shell.rs`: **10 px ×1, 11 px ×7, 12 px ×12, 13 px ×20, 14 ×2, 15 ×4, 17 ×3, 20 ×1, 22 ×2, 28 ×1**; icons 12–22 px; unsized text (the application sets no default text size) is iced's 16 px, which is what snora's checkbox and radio labels already use. Navigation labels are 11 px (10 on the phone bar); result-card details 11 px; descriptions 11–13 px.

## 1. Part 1 — the scale (one or two commits)

1. `ui/type_scale.rs` with the five steps above and a short doc comment per step saying **what it is for**: `HEADING` 28: the page headline ("Conversion complete!"); `TITLE` 20: section titles and the application name's equivalents; `BODY` 16: everything the user reads or acts on (labels, buttons, descriptions of what a control does, toasts); `SECONDARY` 14: supporting lines (blurbs under a section, card details, navigation labels, the shortcut descriptions); `CAPTION` 13: **only** text that no action depends on (the footer "Logolig — favicon generator", the version-like footnotes, the units next to a number whose meaning the label already carries). Icons: `ui/type_scale.rs` also holds `ICON_SMALL` 16 and `ICON_LARGE` 22 (the existing icon sizes round to these two); replace the literals passed to `icon_element_sized`.
2. **Classify every `.size(...)` use** with these rules, in the table: 10/11/12 → `SECONDARY` (14) when it informs or labels a control; `CAPTION` (13) only if it passes the "nothing depends on it" test (state the reason in the table); 13 → `SECONDARY` or `BODY` by role (**a button label or a label the user must read to act is `BODY`**); 14/15 → `SECONDARY`/`BODY` by role; 17 → `TITLE`; 20/22 → `TITLE`; 28 → `HEADING`. Navigation labels become `SECONDARY` on the desktop sidebar and the phone bar.
3. **The sidebar may grow** to make the Japanese labels fit at 14 px: up to **112 px** wide on the desktop (today 90); the bound is the architect's. If a label still does not fit without wrapping or truncation at 112, **stop and report** (do not shrink the text below the step). The phone bar keeps three equal items.
4. Resolve what the larger text breaks **inside these bounds** and report each decision in the review request: longer cards (the grid may go to two columns on the desktop below a width: say where and why), wrapping rules, the footer line, toast widths. If a fix needs a new design decision (an element moved, a text shortened, a setting removed), **stop and ask**; do not decide silently.
5. The source test and the table are red first: the test fails on the head (list the current literals), green after.

## 2. Part 2 — target sizes (one commit)

Primary controls **≥ 44 px** in both axes: "Download all (ZIP)", "Choose file…" (the chip and the drop-zone card), "← Back", the navigation items (the phone bar items too). **Other controls ≥ 32 px**: the Save buttons, the preview toggle and its pickers' rows, "+ Add custom size" and the custom-size field (the last `hit_area.rs::KNOWN` entry, 23.5 px), "Reset to defaults", the "Advanced settings" header, the toast's close control, the radio-group options. Raise by padding (not by changing text size). **P3 gets two thresholds** (`PRIMARY_MIN` 44, `MIN` 32) with the list of which labels are primary kept **in the test** (a small named list: adding a primary control without listing it is a review question); `KNOWN` is **emptied** (the stale-entry check enforces it).

**The one exception, stated now: snora's own form controls.** `snora::checkbox` rows measure about 30.5 px (the label at 16 px plus snora's padding; `Checkbox` has no padding or size setter) and the radio options about 32 px. The app cannot change snora's padding. **Do not work around it** (no custom widget, no wrapper that fakes the size). Pin what is measured: P3's "others" threshold applies to checkbox rows as **≥ 30** (a named, commented rule in the test: "snora's checkbox row, RFC 034 J22"), the shortfall against 32 is reported in the review request with the measured numbers, and the architect adds the request to the snora draft (`.git-exclude/upstream/snora/send/draft/`). Do not touch the draft.

New property: **the Result screen's scrolling middle is at least 200 px high** at every matrix size and in every state of that screen (7 and 15 cards, preview closed and open, both languages): in `ui_layout_tests/` as its own file; red or green on the head (say which), green at the end. If 360 × 640 cannot meet it with header, footer and bottom bar at the new sizes, **stop and report the numbers** with the options (a smaller footer button padding there, the header and footer on one line), do not weaken 200.
