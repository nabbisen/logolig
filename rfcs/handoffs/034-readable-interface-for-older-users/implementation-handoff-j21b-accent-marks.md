## The problem, from your own measurements

After J21 snora's engine path draws a checked box and a selected radio **blue on Light** (`#4E54FF`, a brighter blue than the primary `#3C47B8`) and **white on Dark**. The themes disagree and Light has two blues. Snora's rule (`snora-widgets-0.65.0/src/form.rs`, `Colors` for the unstyled path): `accent = held_against(&[page, background.weak, background.strong], primary.strong.color)` and the tick is `readable_text(accent, primary.strong.text)`: **the mark follows the theme's `primary.strong`**, and falls back to black/white when that colour does not hold the contrast against all three. So the application decides the mark by what `ui/theme.rs` makes `primary.strong`.
