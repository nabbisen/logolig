## What the spike proved

`corner_threshold` is the *minimum* turn angle that counts as a corner, so a **higher** value smooths more. `Sharp` (80) was identical to `Default` (60) on 90° and acute corners and **rounder** on obtuse corners (a pentagon's 108° corner reads 126° traced, against 112° at 60), with about 1.7× more path segments. The label "Logos and icons, with crisp edges (Sharp)" was therefore false. At **45** `Sharp` is at least as sharp as 60 on all 22 corner fixtures, has no more segments than `Default` on 21 of 22, and avoids the artefacts of 30 and 15 (shallow corners cut). For the other two labels: `Default` is vtracer's own default (true as "the baseline"); on a smooth gradient **all three presets flatten the picture to one colour**, so "gradients" is not supported for `PhotoRich` (its measurable gain is on noisy colour images: 3.87 vs 4.00 error, +4.5 % bytes).
