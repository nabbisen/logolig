## Decision (architect, after the owner's word of 2026-11-26)

The H1 spike recommended keeping `chrono` and I proposed to ship 1.29.0 with its "unmaintained" notice. **The owner answered: "Security is really important."** I read that as overruling the acceptance: **1.29.0 does not ship with a dependency that has an open maintenance notice if a clean replacement exists.** The clean replacement is the one that adds **no package and no `unsafe` code anywhere in our dependencies**: the spike's A3 (std only, 35 lines). The spike's A2 (`time` with local offset: +4 packages and a libc `unsafe` call in a dependency) and A5 (`jiff`) are **excluded** by that rule; A4 (a fixed 1980 stamp) is excluded as it dates every extracted file 1980. The price of A3 is that a ZIP's built-in time field has no time zone, so a UTC stamp is shown as 9 hours earlier (Tokyo) or 8 hours later (Los Angeles) by extractors that read it as local time. **Your task is to measure whether that price can be avoided without any new package, and to take the best variant that costs nothing.**
